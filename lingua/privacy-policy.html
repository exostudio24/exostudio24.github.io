Summary

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Lingua Android app distributed through Google Play. EXO is responsible for the app and for personal information you voluntarily send to us outside the app, such as a support email.

2. Information processed on your device

Lingua stores your interface language, selected proficiency, daily goal, XP, level, streak, practice dates, completed lessons, and identifiers used to prevent a lesson session from being counted twice. This information is processed only in Android’s private app storage and is not transmitted to EXO or third parties.

Android backup is disabled for Lingua. You can erase all Lingua data from Settings in the app, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

3. Information we do not collect

The current version does not collect account details, names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone content, health or financial information, messages, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, or app activity. It has no account, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, remote crash reporting, push notifications, purchases, subscriptions, social features, or user-generated content.

4. Permissions, tracking, and advertising

Lingua does not request sensitive device permissions. It does not use the Android Advertising ID, track users across apps or websites, or sell or share personal information for advertising.

5. Support communications

If you email us, we receive information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, and attachments. We use it to respond, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, and maintain necessary business or legal records. We do not use support correspondence for advertising.

6. Sharing and international transfers

The app does not send app or learning data to EXO or third parties. We may disclose support correspondence only when necessary to operate our email service, comply with law, protect rights and safety, or complete a corporate transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. Retention and deletion

Learning data remains on your device until you reset it, clear app storage, or uninstall Lingua. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, maintain records, prevent abuse, or comply with law. To request deletion of a support message, email hello@exo.ltd.

8. Security

Lingua relies on Android’s application sandbox for local data and disables Android cloud backup. No storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure, but we minimize risk by not transmitting app data.

9. Children’s privacy

Lingua is designed for adults and is not directed to children under 18. The app does not knowingly collect personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent personal information to our support address may contact us to request deletion.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, and to complain to a competent authority. The app holds no server-side learner profile. Email us to exercise an applicable right relating to support correspondence.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Lingua, applicable law, or our service providers change. We will revise the date above and provide any additional notice required by law.
