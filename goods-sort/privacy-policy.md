## Summary

- Goods Sort works offline and does not require an account.
- The current release does not collect or share personal data.
- Game progress, settings, virtual currency, boosters, and local diagnostic
  events remain in the app's private storage on your device.
- The current release contains no active advertising, analytics,
  crash-reporting, cloud, or in-app-purchase SDK.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to the Goods Sort mobile game distributed through Apple
App Store and Google Play. EXO is the data controller where applicable.

## 2. Information processed on your device

Goods Sort stores level progress, stars, virtual coins, boosters, missions,
daily streaks, reward identifiers, language, audio, haptic, contrast, and
reduced-motion preferences locally on your device. It may also retain a bounded
local queue of non-personal gameplay events to support on-device quality
diagnostics.

This information is not transmitted to EXO or third parties in the current
release and is not associated with your identity.

## 3. Information we do not collect

The current release does not collect account information, precise or
approximate location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone content, health
data, payment-card data, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers. It
does not provide accounts, cloud sync, chat, user-generated content, push
notifications, advertising, analytics, or remote crash reporting.

## 4. Permissions, tracking, and advertising

Goods Sort does not request sensitive device permissions or tracking
authorization. It does not use IDFA or Android Advertising ID and does not sell
or share personal information for targeted or cross-context behavioral
advertising.

The codebase includes disabled interfaces that may support advertising or
purchases in a future version. They do not transmit data in the current
release. We will update the app, this policy, store disclosures, and applicable
privacy manifests before enabling any such service.

## 5. Payments

The current release offers no paid products or subscriptions. Apple or Google
may independently process store, download, device, diagnostic, or payment
information under their own policies. EXO does not receive full payment-card
details from Apple or Google.

## 6. Sharing and international transfers

Because the app does not transmit gameplay or personal data to EXO or
third-party providers, EXO does not share or internationally transfer app data
in the current release. Information you voluntarily send to our support email
is processed only to answer your request and maintain necessary business and
legal records.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the app or erase its
app data. Reinstallation or backup behavior may depend on your device and store
settings. Goods Sort has no account, server profile, or cloud save to delete.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to
resolve the request, prevent abuse, maintain business records, or comply with
law. You may request deletion by emailing **admin@exo.ltd**.

## 8. Security

Goods Sort uses the operating system's application sandbox for local data. No
storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure, but we limit processing
to what is necessary to operate the game.

## 9. Children's privacy

Goods Sort is a general-audience casual puzzle game and is not directed to
children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from
children. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent information to our
support address may contact us to request deletion.

## 10. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, including the EEA, United Kingdom, California, and
other jurisdictions, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict,
object to, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, and to appeal or
complain to a privacy authority. We do not sell or share personal information.
The current release holds no server-side player data. Email **admin@exo.ltd**
to exercise an applicable right; we will respond as required by law.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, law, or service providers change. We
will revise the "Last updated" date and provide additional notice when legally
required.
