1. Scope

This policy applies to the Goods Sort mobile game distributed through Apple App Store and Google Play. EXO is the data controller where applicable.

2. Information processed on your device

Goods Sort stores level progress, stars, virtual coins, boosters, missions, daily streaks, reward identifiers, language, audio, haptic, contrast, and reduced-motion preferences locally on your device. It may also retain a bounded local queue of non-personal gameplay events for on-device quality diagnostics. This information is not transmitted to EXO or third parties and is not associated with your identity.

3. Information we do not collect

The current release does not collect account information, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone content, health data, payment-card data, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers. It provides no accounts, cloud sync, chat, user-generated content, push notifications, advertising, analytics, or remote crash reporting.

4. Permissions, tracking, and advertising

Goods Sort does not request sensitive permissions or tracking authorization. It does not use IDFA or Android Advertising ID and does not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising. Disabled code interfaces may support advertising or purchases in a future version; they do not transmit data now. We will update this policy and all store disclosures before enabling such services.

5. Payments

The current release offers no paid products or subscriptions. Apple or Google may independently process store, download, device, diagnostic, or payment information under their own policies. EXO does not receive full payment-card details from them.

6. Sharing and international transfers

Because the app transmits no gameplay or personal data, EXO does not share or internationally transfer app data. Information voluntarily sent to our support email is processed only to answer the request and maintain necessary business or legal records.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you delete the app or erase its app data. Reinstallation or backup behavior may depend on your device and store settings. Goods Sort has no account, server profile, or cloud save. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary. Request deletion at admin@exo.ltd.

8. Security

Goods Sort uses the operating system's application sandbox for local data. No storage method is guaranteed completely secure, but processing is limited to what is necessary to operate the game.

9. Children's privacy

Goods Sort is a general-audience casual puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian may contact us to request deletion of information sent to support.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, including the EEA, United Kingdom, California, and other jurisdictions, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, and to appeal or complain to a privacy authority. We do not sell or share personal information and hold no server-side player data. Email us to exercise an applicable right.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the app, law, or service providers change. We will revise the date above and provide additional notice when legally required.
