Privacy Policy

Clap Find My Phone (com.exo.clapfindmyphone) — last updated July 19, 2026.

Summary: we collect no data

Clap Find My Phone does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app requires no account, contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and makes no network requests with your data.

Microphone

The app uses your device's microphone solely to detect clap and whistle sounds while the listening screen is active. All audio is analyzed on your device in real time. No audio is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted off your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that change these practices (for example, advertising), this policy will be updated before release.
