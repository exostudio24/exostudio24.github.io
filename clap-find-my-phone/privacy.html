Privacy Policy

Clap Find My Phone (com.exo.clapfindmyphone) — last updated July 19, 2026.

Microphone

The app uses your device's microphone solely to detect clap and whistle sounds while the listening screen is active. All audio is analyzed on your device in real time. No audio is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted off your device.

Data We Collect

The app itself does not require an account and does not collect personal information. We do not have access to your contacts, photos, location, or files.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's Privacy Policy. You can limit ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
