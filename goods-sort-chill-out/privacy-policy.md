# Privacy Policy — Goods Sort Chill Out

Effective and last updated: August 12, 2026

Goods Sort Chill Out is an offline game. The current version does not require an account, display advertising, use analytics SDKs, access sensitive device permissions, or collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Game progress and preferences stay on your device.

Also see our [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md).

## 1. Who we are

This policy describes how EXO Technologies Global Limited ("EXO," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in the iOS application Goods Sort Chill Out (the "App"). EXO is the data controller where applicable.

## 2. Information we collect

The current version does not collect personal data. It has no account, login, chat, user-generated content, cloud sync, ads, analytics, crash-reporting SDK, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or push notifications.

The App stores game progress, coin balance, booster counts, and audio/haptic preferences locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to EXO or third parties. Delete the App to remove it, subject to Apple's backup behavior.

If you voluntarily email support, we receive the information you include. We use it only to answer and resolve your request. Do not send sensitive personal information.

## 3. Use, sharing, and tracking

We do not profile users, sell or share personal information, serve targeted ads, track users across apps or websites, access IDFA, or disclose App data to data brokers or ad networks. Support information is used to respond, protect the App and our rights, and comply with law.

## 4. Third-party services

Apple distributes the App and may independently process store, download, device, diagnostic, or payment-related information under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). EXO does not receive payment-card details from Apple.

## 5. Retention, security, and transfers

Local game data remains on your device until removed. Support messages are kept only as reasonably necessary to resolve requests, keep business records, prevent abuse, and comply with law. Support messages may be processed where EXO or its providers operate, using appropriate safeguards where required.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, and to appeal or complain to a privacy authority. We do not sell or share App data or use it for targeted advertising. Contact us to exercise a right.

## 7. Children

The App is not directed specifically to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent information to support may contact us for deletion.

## 8. Accounts, permissions, and notifications

The App has no accounts to delete, does not request sensitive device permissions or tracking permission, and does not send push notifications.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy as the App, law, or our practices change. If a future version adds data processing, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures.
