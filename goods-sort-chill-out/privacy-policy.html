Privacy Policy

Goods Sort Chill Out · Effective and last updated: August 12, 2026

Summary: Goods Sort Chill Out is an offline game. The current version does not require an account, display advertising, use analytics SDKs, access sensitive device permissions, or collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Game progress and preferences stay on your device.

Also see our Terms of Service.

1. Who we are

This policy describes how EXO Technologies Global Limited ("EXO," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in the iOS application Goods Sort Chill Out (the "App"). EXO is the data controller where applicable.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Personal data

The current version of the App does not collect personal data. There is no account registration, login, chat, user-generated content, cloud synchronization, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting SDK, in-app purchase, subscription, or push notification service.

2.2 Information stored locally

The App stores game progress, coin balance, booster counts, and audio/haptic preferences locally on your device. This data is used only to preserve your game state and is not transmitted to EXO or third parties. You can remove it by deleting the App or clearing its local data through device features where available.

2.3 Support communications

If you voluntarily email us for support, we receive the information you include, such as your email address and message. We use it only to answer and resolve your request. Do not send sensitive personal information.

3. How we use information

Because the App itself does not collect personal data, we do not profile users or use App activity for advertising. Information sent directly to support is processed to respond to your request, protect the App and our rights, and comply with law. Where the GDPR applies, the legal bases are our legitimate interest in providing support and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Sharing, sale, advertising, and tracking

We do not sell or share personal information, serve targeted ads, track users across apps or websites, access IDFA, or use the Android Advertising ID in the current iOS version. We do not disclose App data to data brokers or ad networks.

5. Third-party services and stores

Apple distributes the App and may independently process store, download, device, diagnostic, or payment-related information under Apple's Privacy Policy. EXO does not receive payment-card details from Apple. External websites you choose to visit operate under their own policies.

6. Data retention and security

Local game data remains on your device until the App or its data is removed. Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain business records, prevent abuse, and comply with law. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. International transfers

The App does not transmit personal data to EXO. If you contact us, your message may be processed in locations where EXO or its service providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, and to appeal or complain to a privacy authority. California and other US residents may also have rights concerning sale, sharing, and targeted advertising; we do not engage in those activities for this App. Contact us to exercise a right. We may need to verify your request and will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed specifically to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App has no account, advertising, analytics, chat, or data transmission, it is designed to be usable without providing personal data. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent information to support may contact us for deletion.

10. Account deletion, permissions, and notifications

The App has no accounts to delete, does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, health, or tracking permission, and does not send push notifications. Deleting the App removes its local game data from the device, subject to Apple's backup behavior.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, law, or our practices change. We will update the date above and provide additional notice when required. If a future version adds analytics, advertising, online services, purchases, or additional data processing, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before or when that processing begins.
